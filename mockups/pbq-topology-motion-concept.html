The drag carries no transition at all, and that is the deliberate part. A dragged element is already following the pointer at display rate; adding a transition on top of that fights the finger and reads as lag. So the dragged chip gets transition: none outright, and every bit of motion is spent on the drop — a FLIP that measures the device's palette position and its landing slot, then animates the delta so it visibly travels rather than teleporting. Same technique as the order type's reveal, applied to a different beat.

The receptive-zone state is not hover-gated on purpose. Touch fires no dragover, so a hover-only drop affordance leaves tapping users with no idea where a selected device can go. Arming a device lights every zone, on every input method.
