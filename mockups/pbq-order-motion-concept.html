Reveal correct order—FLIProws physically travel to their right positions — see below

The FLIP is the whole point of this type. The real engine currently swaps innerHTML after 1500ms, so the corrected sequence just appears — the user sees a different list, not their list being fixed. Measuring each row's position before and after, then animating the delta, means the steps visibly travel to where they belonged. Only the rows that actually moved get highlighted, so the teaching signal is "these two were swapped", not "here is the answer again". That is the difference between showing a correction and showing a correction happening.
