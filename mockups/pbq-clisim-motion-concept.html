The stagger is deliberately capped, and deliberately off-grid. Terminal output runs long — ipconfig /all here is 14 lines. At BRAND's 60ms reveal unit that is 840ms of drip before the last line lands, which reads as a slow app rather than a fast machine. So terminal lines use 34ms and only the first six stagger; everything after lands together. This is the one place in the app where the 60ms unit is the wrong answer, because the thing being simulated has its own physics — a real terminal dumps a buffer, it does not reveal prose.
