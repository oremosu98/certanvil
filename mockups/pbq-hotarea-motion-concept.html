Every region is a real <button>, and that is the load-bearing decision here. The engine currently hand-rolls tabindex, role="button" and Enter/Space handlers onto <div>s and SVG <g>s. A button gets all of that for free, gives the tooltip a :focus-visible path so it is reachable on touch, and takes a min-height: 44px without a fight. Hot-area is the easiest surface in the app to ship a 20px tap target on — the topology sub-shape in particular renders regions over a diagram, where it is tempting to size them to the artwork rather than the finger.
