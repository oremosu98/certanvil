# Lift B3c2 (Why-Not on phones) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** At phone width, Why-Not's entry, question, reason step, round verdict and session verdict look and flow like the approved E2E drawing, with leaving mid-session asking first, and no change to generation, the quality gate, scoring, history, milestones or the Pro gate.

**Architecture:** A new eager `defer` module, `features/ios-whynot.js`, holds pure model/view pairs (entry, reasons, round verdict, session verdict) and small hosts that render into today's roots (`#iwn-entry` new, `#wn-round-root`, `#wn-verdict-root`). `features/flagship-drills.js` hands phones to it from its five Why-Not renderers and records two facts the open rows need. The topic sheet is B3c1's `iosGauntlet.pickTopic`; the leave prompt is `iosSheet`.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS (IIFE, `window` exports), CSS in `lift-ios.css` (phone only), UAT (`tests/uat/*.js`, vm sandbox via `_fnBody`), Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-ios-lift-b3c2-design.md` (approved). E2E: june-e2e `mockups/cert-ios-whynot.html` @ `a5e62ee7`, states `entry`, `entry-free`, `entry-pick`, `loading`, `question`, `reason`, `reason-picked`, `reason-wrong`, `verdict`, `verdict-open`, `session`, `leave` (founder approved in the Simulator, 25 Oct).

## Global Constraints

- Phone only (`max-width: 899px`). Desktop Why-Not renders and behaves exactly as today, including leaving without asking.
- `app.js` stays at its 8301-line ratchet; no inline `on*=` (Sec-P7); `data-act` delegation or `addEventListener`.
- Escape every topic, question, option, reason, fake, note and explanation string. The callout's `<b>` wraps already-escaped option text.
- Copy, exactly (today's strings plus the drawing's):
  - Entry: title "Why-Not"; free users only the "Pro" lock pill in the header; headline `Prove the wrong answers <em>wrong</em>`; lede "The right answer is one point. Why the others lose is the other three."; "Today's target"; why "Your pick" / "Your weakest topic right now" / "A good place to start"; "Pick a different topic"; section "Three rounds"; strip "Round 1" · "Round 2" · "Round 3"; dock "Start Why-Not".
  - Question (Lift A quiz, phones): `#q-label` "Round N of 3", `#prog-fill` width `round(N*100/3)%`; today's chip "Why-Not · topic · Round N of 3".
  - Reason step: strip `Why-Not · <b>topic</b> · Round N of 3`; answered line `You answered <b>X</b>. Right. Now earn the other three points.` / `The answer was <b>X</b>. The reasons below still score.`; card letter + option text, "Why does it lose?", three reasons; note label "Right reason" / "Wrong reason" + today's note; bar labels "Answer" · "B" · "C" · "D" (the round's three wrong letters); dock "Next: why does C lose?" (letter of the next wrong option) / "See the round verdict", then "Leave session".
  - Round verdict: strip as the reason step; `You earned <em>N of 4</em>`; today's callout; section "Your four points"; rows "The answer: X" (sub "Picked first time" / "Missed on the first pick") and each wrong option's text (sub "Option L"), pills "Right" / "Wrong"; opened: answer row "Your first pick" + the option picked on the quiz + explanation; option rows "Your reason" (only when wrong) + the picked reason, "True reason" + the true reason, the note; dock "Next round" / "Session verdict", then "Leave session".
  - Session verdict: kicker "Why-Not · topic"; `Session: <em>N of 12</em>`; a 12-point meter; "3 questions answered toward today's goal."; section "Your three rounds"; rows "Round N" with pill "N of 4" (`ok` at 4, neutral otherwise); dock "Next target", then "Back to Drills" / "Back to Home".
  - Leave sheet: title "Leave Why-Not?", body "Your rounds so far won't count.", primary "Keep going", destructive "Leave".
- No em-dashes and no arrow glyphs in phone labels.
- CSS prefix `iwn-`. Plain top padding, never `env(safe-area-inset-top)`. Reveals rest visible and animate from hidden with fill `backwards` (UAT 481 guard). Motion per BRAND §6 and the visual-pass decisions: entry and verdicts reveal once; options 2–3 of a round swap without a re-stagger (`#wn-round-root.iwn-static`); after a pick the note fades in from `translateY(4px)` over 200ms with an 80ms delay; Next keeps its slot (`.iwn-next.wait` = `visibility: hidden; opacity: 0`) and fades in over 180ms with a 140ms delay, its press scale never delayed; the live bar segment uses `transform: scaleY(1.4)`; rows open in 220ms and close instantly; sheets reuse B2/B3c1 timings; press `scale(0.97)` (reasons and rows `0.99`); hover gated; reduced motion collapses to fades.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px (reasons ≥ 52px, rows ≥ 56px).
- Ship as v8.191.0 only on the founder's "ship it".

## Review Focus

1. **Double-tap on a reason:** one pick is scored, the note and Next show once. (Task 3 test `a double-tap on a reason scores once`.)
2. **Rotating across 899px on the reason step or a verdict:** the right layout redraws in place with the same option and picks. (Task 3 test `crossing 899px keeps the reason step`.)
3. **Keep going in the leave sheet:** nothing changes; Leave lands on Drills and a normal quiz afterwards shows "Question 1 of N". (Task 3 test `leave sheet: Keep going stays, Leave exits`.)
4. **Option, reason or note text with `&` and `<`:** escaped on every phone screen. (Task 1 UAT.)
5. **The forge misfires:** back on the phone entry with today's toast, and Start works again. (Task 2 test `a misfire returns to the phone entry`.)
