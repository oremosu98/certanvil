# Lift B2 (Exam and exam results) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** At phone width the exam, its End dialog, leaving, and the exam results look and flow exactly like the founder-approved E2E, on the real exam engine.

**Architecture:** The exam page keeps its markup and engine. `lift-ios.css` restyles it (`ix-` section), and `features/exam.js` gains small phone-only hooks: short labels, a dock Flag, the End-dialog grid, and an in-app sheet in place of `confirm()` at phone width. Results get a phone renderer, `features/ios-exam.js` (pure `_iosExamResultsModel` / `_iosExamResultsHtml`, UAT vm-testable), rendered into `#ios-exam-results` from the data `submitExam()` already computes.

**Tech Stack:** Vanilla JS IIFEs, CSS gated at `(max-width: 899px)`, UAT (`tests/uat/*.js`, vm sandbox), Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-ios-lift-b2-design.md`. E2E (source of truth): branch `feat/cert-ios-e2e`, `mockups/cert-ios-exam.html` (states `?v=paused|jump|leave|pbq`) and `mockups/cert-ios-exam-results.html` (`?v=toast`), commits `597e06e8` + `47147981`.

## Global Constraints
- Fidelity rule: same layout, copy, spacing, type, colour and motion as the E2E; any deviation goes to the founder before it ships.
- Phone only, `(max-width: 899px)`; desktop renders exactly as today (the one shared fix: the literal `·` in the submit eyebrow).
- `app.js` stays at 8301 lines.
- CSS ported with `scratchpad/port_css.py` (`ix-` exam, `ie-` results); BRAND §6 motion (`--ease cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)`, press 150–160ms, sheet 360ms in / 200ms out, chart sweep 800–1100ms); reduced motion → fades.
- Theme tokens only, no hardcoded hex; the results score card follows the theme (founder, 21 Oct).
- Touch targets ≥ 44pt (40px CSS at the 1.10 zoom); jump grid 7 columns.
- No em-dashes in on-screen copy.
- B1 lessons, applied: a `position:fixed` screen needs the page-enter animation off; a hidden hook must not carry a class whose `display` beats `[hidden]` (e.g. `.btn`); a bare `showPage` does not render, so tests arrive by the real path.
- Cross-platform: Desktop (unchanged), Safari/WebKit, mobile web at 390/402, iOS Capacitor in the Simulator.
- Branch `feat/lift-b2`; ships as v8.186.0.

## Review Focus
1. **Resume and pause across the restyle:** a paused exam resumed from Home shows the paused cover with its time left; a running one shows the question and dock. (Task 3)
2. **Sim Lab questions in the exam:** the console mounts inside the restyled card, Reset works, and the dock stays usable. (Task 3)
3. **A perfect exam or no wrong answers:** results show no "Drill the gap" button (nothing to drill) and no empty Sim Lab block when the exam had no PBQs. (Task 2)
4. **Another cert (Sec+, different scale/pass mark):** the score card, meter tick and label use that cert's `EXAM_MIN/MAX/PASS_SCORE` and name. (Task 2)
5. **Leaving to a specific page:** after "Leave" the app lands on the page that was asked for (Home via `goSetup`, any other via `showPage`), and the exam is offered for resume. (Task 5)

### Task 2: Results model and view

**Files:**
- Modify: `features/ios-exam.js`
- Test: `tests/uat/495-ios-exam-results.js`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces `_iosExamResultsModel(d)` where `d = { scaled, minScore, maxScore, passScore, certLabel, total, correct, wrong, skipped, pct, pauses, pausedMs, domains:[{key,label,correct,total,pct}], pbqs:[{title,topic,correct,total,fraction,skipped}], wrongTopics:[{topic,domain,wrong}] }` returning
  `{ passed, gap, over, headline:{lead,em}, sub, label, tags:[{kind:'pass'|'fail'|'mode', text}], msg, note, meter:{fill,tick,min,pass,max}, tiles:[{n,l,kind}], domains:[{label,detail,pct,tone:'pass'|'mid'|'miss'|'none'}], pbq:null|{right,count,rows:[{title,topic,verdict:'Right'|'Partly'|'Wrong'|'Skipped',tone,detail}]}, gapTopics:[topic] }`.
- Produces `_iosExamResultsHtml(m) -> string` with `ie-` classes from the E2E.

Copy and rules (exact):
- `gap = passScore - scaled`, `over = scaled - passScore`; `passed = scaled >= passScore`.
- Headline: passed → `Pass mark` / `cleared.`; failed with `gap <= 100` → `So` / `close.`; else `More` / `work needed.`
- Sub: passed → `{over} points over the line.`; failed and 1 or 2 domains in the miss tone → `{gap} points off a pass. The gap is {one domain|two domains}, not the whole exam.`; otherwise failed → `{gap} points off a pass. Start with {lowest domain}.`
- Score card label `Scaled score · {certLabel}`; tags: `Pass` or `Fail`, plus `Paused {N} min` when `pauses > 0` (N = max(1, round(pausedMs/60000))).
- Msg: failed → `<b>{gap} points</b> from the line, and most of it is {A} and {B}.` (the two lowest domains with questions; one name when only one); passed → `<b>{over} points</b> above the line.`
- Note under the meter (kept from desktop, honesty): `An estimate: the real exam's scaling isn't published.`
- Meter: `fill = (scaled-min)/(max-min)`, `tick = (pass-min)/(max-min)` as percentages; legend `{min}` / `Pass {pass}` / `{max}`.
- Tiles: Correct (good), Wrong (bad), Skipped, Raw `{pct}%`.
- Domains sorted by pct high to low, domains with no questions last; tone ≥70 pass, ≥60 mid, else miss, no questions none; detail `{c} of {t} correct` or `Not in this exam`.
- Sim Lab block only when `pbqs.length`: kicker `Performance-based · {right} of {count} right`; verdict Right (fraction 1), Partly (0 < fraction < 1), Wrong (0), Skipped; detail `{correct} of {total} steps right.`
- `gapTopics`: topics from `wrongTopics` in the two lowest domains, most wrong first, at most 4; empty → the view has no Drill the gap button.

- [ ] **Step 1:** Write UAT cases with fixtures for: a near-miss fail (scaled 620, pass 720 → "So close.", two miss domains → "The gap is two domains"), a far fail (scaled 400 → "More work needed.", "Start with …"), a pass (760 → "Pass mark cleared.", "40 points over the line."), Sec+ scale (min 100, max 900, pass 750 → tick 81.3%), paused (pausedMs 240000 → tag `Paused 4 min`), PBQ rows (fractions 1, 0.5, 0, skipped → Right/Partly/Wrong/Skipped, kicker `1 of 4 right`), no PBQs → `pbq === null` and no `ie-srow` in HTML, no wrong topics → no `drill-gap` button, `gapTopics` capped at 4 and limited to the two lowest domains, a topic `A & <B>` escaped in HTML.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run; expect FAIL (`_iosExamResultsModel is not defined`).
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement both pure functions.
- [ ] **Step 4:** Run; expect PASS.
- [ ] **Step 5:** Commit `feat(B2): exam results phone model and view`.
