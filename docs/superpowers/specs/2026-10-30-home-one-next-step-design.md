# One clear next step on Home

From [[2026-10-30-persona-adhd-gaps]] gap 7. The founder approved it on 30 Oct. It ships in the
bundle with the streak, quiz resume and honest copy.

## The problem
Home has two "do this next" buttons, Recommended next and the momentum card's "Pick up … Practise it",
and they can point at different topics. After days away, the suggestion is still a 15-minute,
exam-level set.

## The design
1. **One main action.** Recommended next is the only "do this next" button. The momentum card loses
   its "Practise it" button and its "Pick up" line. When the recommendation is the weak-spot set, its
   subtitle names the pick-up topic: "10 questions, exam level, on Ethernet Basics (around 46%)."
2. **Ease back in after a break.** Away 2 or more days, with no reviews due and no saved exam or quiz,
   the card becomes "5-min Warmup": "Ease back in. 5 questions, foundational." It starts the existing
   Warmup.
3. **The order of the card:**
   1. a saved exam or quiz to resume
   2. reviews due
   3. how did your exam go (see [[2026-10-30-exam-outcome-design]])
   4. first quiz
   5. Warmup after a break
   6. weak spots
4. Phone only. Desktop Home is unchanged.

## Rejected approaches
- **Folding the study menus behind "More ways to study":** not asked for. Home's length stays.
- **Keeping "Pick up" as a second button:** two answers to "what now?" is the problem.
